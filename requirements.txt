Your milestone report should include all sections listed below:
Executive Summary (5 points)
The executive summary provides an overview of your report. It describes the problem being solved, why it is important, and how you plan on solving it. It will be between one-third and two-thirds of a page. It should be read as a “pitch”, endorsing the problem and your solution. However, it should stick to the facts. There should be a single sentence that summarizes the project objective. Write this sentence in bold. 
Proposed Solution and Implementation (10 points)
Provide an overview of your solution. Address the comments and feedback that you received in the project proposal. This is the time to really formalize your approach! Note that a high-level drawing is almost always useful. You should discuss the major components of your design. This section should be detailed. 

Address the following points:

What are the inputs and outputs to your system?
How does information flow through your system (input/output of each module)?
What is your feature extraction and modeling approach?

Generative AI Policy
Learning to use AI tools is important for all of us.  When used properly, these tools can enhance our work; when used improperly, it can border on plagiarism, or simply be incorrect. If you have used AI on anything you submit for EECS 448, please include an explanation as to: (1) state which model you used; (2) include the prompt(s) that you gave the model; (3) explain your reasoning as to why you agree (or disagree) with the response; (4) mention any incorrect responses or erroneous behavior that you may have seen. An example of how to document generative AI responses is linked.

Any use of generative AI models without this documentation will be treated as an honor code violation.

Things we look for to detect AI use:

Vague terminology that sounds professional but do not have meaning
Undefined terms that require a definition to understand
We pay attention to the ways in which code is documented – tools like Claude and Codex have a specific form of documentation

If you have used AI, document your use, and there will not be any problems! If you use AI for writing, I highly recommend taking the generated language and rephrasing it to be in your own voice, and ensuring all terminology is understandable (I do this for all of my AI-generated or AI-supported writing!). Make sure everything you state is defensible – as you saw in the pre-proposal, we will take off points if things are claimed, not cited, or terminology is used that does not make sense.
